Customer Support (CS) Cycle

Rhythm and cadence for fresh coffee, restocking, and keeping baristas planted.

08:00

Every CS Cycle, Do In Order:

1
Brew Fresh Coffee: Rotate Pike Place, Dark Roast, or Blonde. Reset brew timer.
2
Restock Line Signals: Ice bins filled, whole/2%/oat milks restocked, cups & lids replenished.
3
Lobby & Condiment Sweep: Wipe handoff plane, check trash cans, sanitize café tables.
4
Cycle Dishes: Run 1 rack through Hobart or load sanitize sink so bar never piles up.

The LATTE Framework

Starbucks' world-class service recovery model for turning upsets into loyal guests.

L
Listen: Focus on the customer without interrupting. Let them explain what went wrong.
A
Acknowledge: Validate their emotions: "I completely understand your frustration, and I appreciate you bringing this to my attention."
T
Take Action: Remake the beverage right away with top priority or offer an alternative. Empower baristas to hand out a recovery card if appropriate.
T
Thank: "Thank you for letting us make that right for you today."
E
Encourage Return: Warmly invite them back: "Have a wonderful afternoon and we will see you tomorrow morning!"
